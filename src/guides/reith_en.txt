# Bidding guide: Reith points (6-4-3-2-1)
Count A=6, K=4, Q=3, J=2, 10=1. The deck holds 64 points.
This guide uses a natural five-card-major method, similar to Standard American. The point ranges are the 4-3-2-1 ranges multiplied by 1.6 (64 / 40) and rounded, so they are approximate.

## Opening bids
- 0–18 points: [P], or a weak two or a preempt.
- [1C] [1D] [1H] [1S]: 19–34 points. Open [1H] or [1S] with five or more cards in the suit. Without a five-card major, open the longer minor: [1D] with four or more diamonds, otherwise [1C].
- [1NT]: 24–28 points, balanced hand.
- [2C]: 35 or more points, any shape. Artificial and forcing.
- [2D] [2H] [2S]: weak two, 8–18 points with a good six-card suit.
- [2NT]: 32–34 points, balanced hand.
- [3C] [3D] [3H] [3S]: preempt, less than an opening hand with a good seven-card suit.

## Responses to one of a suit
- 0–9 points: [P].
- 10–15 points: raise partner's major one level ([1H] – [2H]) with three or more cards. Otherwise bid a new suit at the one level with four or more cards, or [1NT].
- 16–20 points: invitational. Jump raise partner's major ([1S] – [3S]) with three or more cards, or bid a new suit. A new suit at the two level needs 16 or more points.
- 21 or more points: game forcing. Bid a new suit, and continue until game ([3NT], [4H], [4S]).

## Opener's rebid
- 19–23 points: [1NT] with a balanced hand, a single raise, or a rebid of your own suit.
- 24–28 points: a jump raise, or a jump rebid of your own suit.
- 29–34 points: a jump in a new suit, or [2NT] with a balanced 29–31 points.

## Responses to [1NT]
- 0–12 points: [P], or a transfer with a five-card major.
- [2C] (Stayman): 13 or more points with a four-card major. Opener answers [2D] without a four-card major, otherwise [2H] or [2S].
- [2D] shows five or more hearts, [2H] shows five or more spades (transfers). Opener bids the next suit.
- [2NT]: 13–15 points, invitational.
- [3NT]: 16–25 points, to play.

## Responses to [2C]
- [2D]: waiting, 0–12 points.
- [2H] [2S] [3C] [3D]: 13 or more points with a good five-card suit.
- [2NT]: 13 or more points, balanced hand.

## Responses to a weak two
- Raise partner's suit to make the auction harder for the opponents.
- [2NT]: 24 or more points, asks opener to describe the hand.

## Competitive bids
- An overcall at the one level: 13–26 points with a good five-card suit. At the two level: 16–26 points.
- [1NT] overcall: 24–29 points, balanced, with a stopper in the opponents' suit.
- [X] (takeout): 19 or more points, short in the opponents' suit, with support for the other suits; or any hand with 27 or more points.
- [XX] after partner's opening is doubled: 16 or more points.

## Targets for the partnership
- 40 points: game in [3NT], [4H] or [4S].
- 46 points: game in [5C] or [5D].
- 53 points: small slam.
- 59 points: grand slam.
- [4NT] (Blackwood) asks for aces: [5C] 0 or 4, [5D] 1, [5H] 2, [5S] 3.
