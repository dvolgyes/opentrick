# Bidding guide: Four Aces points (3-2-1-½)
Count A=3, K=2, Q=1, J=½. The deck holds 26 points.
This guide uses a natural five-card-major method, similar to Standard American. The point ranges are the 4-3-2-1 ranges multiplied by 0.65 (26 / 40) and rounded to half points, so they are approximate.

## Opening bids
- 0–7½ points: [P], unless a weak two or a preempt fits.
- 3½–7½ points: weak two, [2D] [2H] [2S]. A six-card suit with two of A K Q or three of A K Q J 10, and no four-card side major.
- 3½–7 points: preempt, [3C] [3D] [3H] [3S]. A seven-card suit with the same top cards as a weak two.
- 8–14 points: [1C] [1D] [1H] [1S]. [1H] or [1S] needs five or more cards in the suit; with two five-card suits, open the higher. Without a five-card major, open the longer minor: [1D] with 4–4 in the minors, [1C] with 3–3.
- 10–11 points: [1NT], balanced: at least two cards in each suit and at most one doubleton (4333, 4432, 5332).
- 13–14 points: [2NT], balanced as for [1NT].
- 14½ or more points: [2C], any shape. Artificial and forcing. With a balanced 14½–16 points, rebid [2NT] after partner's answer.
- 16½–18 points: [3NT], balanced as for [1NT], instead of [2C].

## Responses to one of a suit
- 0–3½ points: [P].
- 4–6 points: raise partner's major one level ([1H] – [2H]) with three or more cards. Otherwise bid a new suit at the one level with four or more cards, or [1NT].
- 6½–8 points: invitational. Jump raise partner's major ([1S] – [3S]) with three or more cards, or bid a new suit. A new suit at the two level needs 6½ or more points.
- 8½ or more points: game forcing. Bid a new suit, and continue until game ([3NT], [4H], [4S]).

## Opener's rebid
- 8–9½ points: [1NT] with a balanced hand, a single raise, or a rebid of your own suit.
- 10–11 points: a jump raise, or a jump rebid of your own suit.
- 11½–14 points: a jump in a new suit, or [2NT] with a balanced 11½–12½ points.

## Responses to [1NT]
- 0–4½ points: [P], or a transfer with a five-card major.
- [2C] (Stayman): 5 or more points with a four-card major. Opener answers [2D] without a four-card major, otherwise [2H] or [2S].
- [2D] shows five or more hearts, [2H] shows five or more spades (transfers). Opener bids the next suit.
- [2S]: a weak hand with six or more cards in a minor. Opener must bid [3C]; pass with clubs, or bid [3D] with diamonds.
- [2NT]: 5–6 points, invitational.
- [3NT]: 6½–10 points, to play.

## Responses to [2C]
- [2D]: waiting, 0–4½ points.
- [2H] [2S] [3C] [3D]: 5 or more points with a good five-card suit.
- [2NT]: 5 or more points, balanced hand.

## Responses to a weak two
- Raise partner's suit to make the auction harder for the opponents.
- [2NT]: 10 or more points, asks opener to describe the hand.

## Competitive bids
- An overcall at the one level: 5–10½ points with a good five-card suit. At the two level: 6½–10½ points.
- [1NT] overcall: 10–12 points, balanced, with a stopper in the opponents' suit.
- [X] (takeout): 8 or more points, short in the opponents' suit, with support for the other suits; or any hand with 11 or more points.
- [XX] after partner's opening is doubled: 6½ or more points.

## Targets for the partnership
- 16 points: game in [3NT], [4H] or [4S].
- 19 points: game in [5C] or [5D].
- 21½ points: small slam.
- 24 points: grand slam.
- [4NT] (Blackwood) asks for aces: [5C] 0 or 4, [5D] 1, [5H] 2, [5S] 3.
