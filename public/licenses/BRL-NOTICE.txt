OpenTrick distributes the supervised and RL/FSP bidding weights from BRL.
Copyright 2024 Haruka Kita and Sotetsu Koyamada.
Source: https://github.com/harukaki/brl
Original model revision: fdd958ff8adcb44844d70ea2e233bed18d5a7b70
License: Apache-2.0 (BRL-LICENSE.txt in this directory).

The parameters are converted from BRL's Haiku pickle files to little-endian float32 data.
The numerical weights are unchanged.
The raw data distribution is from Nickel Bridge by Brannon Dorsey at revision
92a81b5e81cb7aac2eabc147e82f18e0040b512e, packages/ai/models/{sl,rl-fsp}.bin.
Source: https://github.com/brannondorsey/nickel-bridge
That project's README attributes the weights to BRL under Apache-2.0.
OpenTrick does not incorporate Nickel Bridge application code.

OpenTrick's bid-position encoder adapts the observation layout in PGX 1.4.0
by Sotetsu Koyamada and contributors from Python/JAX to TypeScript.
Source: https://github.com/sotetsuk/pgx/blob/v1.4.0/pgx/bridge_bidding.py
Copyright 2023 The Pgx Authors. All Rights Reserved.
License: Apache-2.0 (PGX-LICENSE.txt in this directory).
